# Community Engagement for Creators and Brands

An audience becomes a community when people feel that their questions, ideas and experiences are treated with respect. Engagement should be genuine, not a tactic for extracting comments.

## 1. Make participation easy

Ask specific, relevant questions. Invite people to share experiences when the topic is appropriate. Do not pressure people to reveal private information.

## 2. Respond with care

Answer questions clearly, thank people for useful feedback and acknowledge uncertainty. If a question needs account-specific support, move it to a secure official channel.

## 3. Set moderation standards

Define what is acceptable and how you will handle spam, harassment, misinformation and personal attacks. Apply rules consistently.

## 4. Protect privacy

Do not publish private messages, personal details, customer records or screenshots without informed permission. Avoid collecting more personal data than necessary.

## 5. Work with feedback

Look for recurring questions and confusion. These can reveal topics for future content or improvements to a product or service.

## 6. Collaborate responsibly

Credit collaborators, agree on deliverables and permissions in advance, and disclose paid or gifted partnerships where required.

## 7. Measure meaningful engagement

Consider the quality of questions, repeat participation, community sentiment and useful customer feedback—not just the number of comments.
