# Short-Form Video Planning for Creators and Brands

Short-form video is a format, not a strategy. A useful video has a clear idea, a suitable audience and a reason to exist.

## 1. Start with one objective

Choose one:
- Explain something.
- Demonstrate a product or process.
- Tell a short story.
- Answer a common question.
- Introduce a point of view.
- Invite a relevant action.

## 2. Use a simple structure

**Opening:** Establish the question or subject.

**Main section:** Deliver the explanation, demonstration or story.

**Close:** Summarise the point or suggest a useful next step.

This structure is flexible. Do not force every video into an identical template.

## 3. Write before recording

Create a short outline:
- Main message.
- Supporting points.
- Visuals or examples.
- Any facts that need checking.
- Call to action, if relevant.

## 4. Record with clarity

Prioritise understandable audio, stable framing, adequate lighting and a clean background. Use accessible text and captions where possible.

## 5. Edit for comprehension

Remove sections that do not help the viewer. Keep transitions and effects purposeful. Do not use editing to imply a result that did not happen.

## 6. Adapt for each platform

Check current aspect ratio, duration, safe-area and music requirements for the destination platform. Avoid assuming that identical exports or watermarked reposts are ideal everywhere.

## 7. Review and improve

Look at viewer retention and feedback where available. Ask whether the opening accurately represented the content and whether the main idea was easy to follow.
