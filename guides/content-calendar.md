# Social Media Content Strategy and Calendar

A content calendar turns goals and ideas into a manageable publishing plan. It should create clarity without making every post feel mechanical.

## 1. Start with goals and audience questions

List the questions customers or followers ask. Connect each question to a business or creator objective.

## 2. Create content pillars

Choose three to five themes that can support multiple useful posts. For example:
- Education.
- Demonstration.
- Stories and perspective.
- Community.
- Product or service information.

## 3. Build a simple calendar

Useful columns include:
- Date.
- Platform.
- Audience.
- Topic or working title.
- Content pillar.
- Format.
- Main message.
- Asset owner.
- Approval status.
- Publishing status.
- Results or notes.

## 4. Plan at a realistic pace

Choose a schedule that fits your resources. Leave space for timely updates, customer questions and experimentation. A calendar is a guide, not a reason to publish weak content.

## 5. Batch related work

Research several topics together, outline scripts, prepare visuals and schedule approved content. Keep a review step for facts, links, rights and brand voice.

## 6. Repurpose with care

A long explanation can inspire a short video, carousel or email. Adapt the structure and examples for each channel instead of copying the same asset everywhere.

## 7. Review the calendar

At the end of each period, note what resonated, what was difficult to produce and which audience questions remain unanswered. Use those observations to plan the next cycle.
