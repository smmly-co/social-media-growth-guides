# Building a Consistent Brand Presence on Social Media

A brand presence is the impression people form from repeated interactions with your business. It is shaped by what you say, how you say it and whether the experience matches your promises.

## 1. Define the brand clearly

Write down:
- Who you serve.
- What problem you help solve.
- What makes your offer distinct.
- What you want customers to remember.
- What evidence supports your claims.

## 2. Establish a voice

Choose a few traits that fit your audience, such as clear, helpful, expert, warm or playful. Define words and claims the brand should avoid.

Consistency does not mean every post must sound identical. It means the brand remains recognisable.

## 3. Create visual guidelines

Set practical rules for:
- Logo use.
- Colours and typography.
- Image and video style.
- Layout and spacing.
- Templates.
- Accessibility and text contrast.

Use templates as a starting point, not as a substitute for good ideas.

## 4. Make claims responsibly

Use accurate product descriptions, current prices and verifiable testimonials. Obtain permission before publishing customer images, reviews or private messages. Disclose paid endorsements and partnerships where required.

## 5. Make profiles and links useful

Keep contact details, business hours, location and important links current. Make it easy for people to understand how to ask a question or purchase.

## 6. Protect reputation

Respond calmly to criticism, correct factual errors and take sensitive customer issues to an appropriate private support channel. Never publish personal information to win an argument.

## 7. Review brand consistency

Periodically review the profile, recent posts, replies and destination pages. Check whether the experience matches the brand promise.
