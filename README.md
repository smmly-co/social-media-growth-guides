## A practical growth framework

1. Know the audience you want to serve.
2. Define what your account or brand should be known for.
3. Choose platforms based on audience and content fit.
4. Publish content that is useful, relevant or entertaining.
5. Make your message and creative easy to understand.
6. Engage with people genuinely and consistently.
7. Review performance and learn from audience response.
8. Improve one part of the process at a time.

## Editorial principles

- Prioritise original, useful and audience-relevant content.
- Do not buy fake engagement or use deceptive growth tactics.
- Do not promise guaranteed reach, followers, leads or revenue.
- Respect copyright, privacy, consent and platform rules.
- Disclose paid partnerships and advertising where required.
- Treat platform features and recommendation systems as changeable; verify current platform guidance before relying on a specific feature.
